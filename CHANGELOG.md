# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

First public release.

- The skill in English, Portuguese and Spanish. English is the reference version; the other two follow its content.
- Step 0: the person's adjustments (add, release, swap alternatives). The skill also looks for them in AGENTS.md and other instruction files, not only in CLAUDE.md.
- Step 1: identity before layout, with a worked example and a rule for when the person already has a brand.
- Habits that give AI work away, each with a reason and an alternative. Slashes, dashes and italic serif are in a separate section, as the author's own pet peeves.
- Approval and fidelity rules apply only when an approved design, brief or existing design is present.
- An "Other formats" table that says how each website rule reads in posters, slides, social posts, banners and covers.
- A final review with a logo test.
- READMEs in three languages, with a version badge, notes for other AI tools and one ready zip per language for the release page.
- Typography has no limit of two families: use the families the concept calls for and justify each by its job (headlines, text, data).
- Removed every rule that compared a project with another one (not repeating fonts, palettes or compositions, the neighbor test, asking for a reference). The only criterion is the identity of the concept itself.
- Step 1 starts by asking for the person's taste in one sentence. With references, they are used; without them, the sentence is enough; with neither, the AI designs only if the person says there is no preference.
- Step 1 asks for three directions derived from the real content of the product when there is no reference, differing in at least three axes (density, color temperature, voice of the typography, language of shape).
- Fonts: a habit for choosing a pair by reflex (with an example list marked as a guess that ages), the job of the small text written before choosing, three candidate pairs tied to the concept, and a font swap test in the final review.
- Step 1 order: the three directions now come right after the taste question and before the four answers; question 3 points to the typography paragraph instead of repeating it.
- Font swap test: swap in the closest reflex pair for the chosen style (for example Poppins 800 for a heavy rounded headline), not the first pair that comes to mind.
- Directions must differ by category and in a perceptible way, not by degree; if two axes of a pair differ only a little, strengthen them or replace the direction.
- Accent check: when choosing a family, confirm it has complete ã, ç, é and ô in the weights that will be used (in all three languages).
- Steps 0 and 1: the question about what to avoid or release and the question about taste go in one message, not two.
- Step 0: if the person answers only one of the two questions, the answer that came is used and the default habits apply to the rest.
